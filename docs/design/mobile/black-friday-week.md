# Black Friday Week — mobile layout trial

The home block stays between Stories and the existing Clean promo. No changes to standalone Stories, Publications, the home header, tabs or other sections. `blackFridayGeometry.ts` uses the 706 px reference: navy header, collection rail overlapping its lower edge by 40 px, 392 px square covers, 16 px gaps, 496 px tall last card with a gray veil and underlined CTA, and a 94 px high outlined bottom CTA (scaled to phone width). The rail scrolls freely and previews the first three active collections. Both CTAs open all active collections; a promo card opens its own product grid. Only the reference geometry is reused, not its media, brands or 75% claim.

**CRM → Contenu → غلاف الواجهة → Black Friday Week**: visibility, two-line heading, FR/AR CTA, optional final-card image (defaults to last product’s photo), add/remove/reorder up to nine discounted photographed catalogue products. The API reads canonical catalogue prices at request time, excludes unavailable items and exposes no draft configuration. The maximum headline is derived from the cards actually shown. A disabled or expired-by-stock section with no offers renders nothing.

The one-time data migration uses the nine named existing ZFR demo SKUs in `src/catalogue/blackFriday.ts`. It applies 15–30% to their actual published prices and preserves the pre-test price as the compare-at value. The update matches the original demo identity and baseline price exactly; it never updates newly authored products or overrides an edited price. Existing databases get a verified pre-migration backup. The initial final-card image comes from a second photo of one of the selected products. No pricing engine/checkout formulas change. Campaign visibility or order does **not** reset catalogue prices; to end the demo sales, restore each desired regular price (the stored compare-at price) in the catalogue editor and clear its compare-at field. Reopening the database does not reapply the trial.

## Campaign landing and product grid

The home CTAs open `/black-friday`, a red active heading and editorial introduction followed by the full list of landscape promo cards. Every card has the same cover and title as its home counterpart, a countdown to its real deadline when configured, and a maximum discount calculated only from its eligible products. A selection opens `/black-friday?view=products&collection=<id>` with a real article count, optional brand filter, price sorting, portrait two-column product tiles, current and struck reference prices and canonical product links. Signed-in users can use catalogue favorites. Unknown, expired or empty selections never fall back to unrelated products. The older grid URL without a selection still shows all active offers. Legacy campaigns without selections use their existing default feature card.

The CRM studio supports up to eight ordered campaign cards sharing the existing pool of up to nine catalogue products. Each card has localized titles, an image, product links, its own optional deadline and a demonstration label. The global deadline, if earlier, wins. Only active cards with at least one eligible offer are published; hidden or expired items do not contribute to the headline percentage. Expiry changes campaign visibility, **not** catalogue prices.

A separate one-time data update adds four demo collections to the untouched nine-SKU selection (three in the home rail and one additional card in the full list). It preserves custom selections, disabled campaigns and customized previous feature cards. The covers are composed from photos of the existing participating products and stored in `assets/media/catalogue/black-friday-demo-v1/`. Demo deadlines are set once at install (7–10 days), not restarted on app or server refresh. This update changes settings JSON only, not the database schema, catalogue or checkout prices.

The inline price editor is available to CRM pricing users for products with a directly authored TND price. It updates `final_price` and `compare_at_price` through an audited, permission- and CSRF-protected write with stale-value checks; the card, product fiche and new cart additions all use the same catalogue price. Imported/cost-calculated prices remain controlled by the existing pricing workflow. An active campaign can only contain products with a photo, stock availability and a real discount. Saving layout, turning the campaign off or expiring it does not write prices.
