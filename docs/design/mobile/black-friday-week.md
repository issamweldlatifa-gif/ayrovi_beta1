# Black Friday Week — mobile layout trial

The home block stays between Stories and the existing Clean promo. No changes to standalone Stories, Publications, the home header, tabs or other sections. `blackFridayGeometry.ts` uses the 706 px reference: navy header, product rail overlapping its lower edge by 40 px, 392 px square covers, 16 px gaps, 496 px tall last card with a gray veil and underlined CTA, and a 94 px high outlined bottom CTA (scaled to phone width). The rail scrolls freely and keeps the next product visible. Both CTAs open the campaign landing; a product opens its own fiche. Only the reference geometry is reused, not its media, brands or 75% claim.

**CRM → Contenu → غلاف الواجهة → Black Friday Week**: visibility, two-line heading, FR/AR CTA, optional final-card image (defaults to last product’s photo), add/remove/reorder up to nine discounted photographed catalogue products. The API reads canonical catalogue prices at request time, excludes unavailable items and exposes no draft configuration. The maximum headline is derived from the cards actually shown. A disabled or expired-by-stock section with no offers renders nothing.

The one-time data migration uses the nine named existing ZFR demo SKUs in `src/catalogue/blackFriday.ts`. It applies 15–30% to their actual published prices and preserves the pre-test price as the compare-at value. The update matches the original demo identity and baseline price exactly; it never updates newly authored products or overrides an edited price. Existing databases get a verified pre-migration backup. The initial final-card image comes from a second photo of one of the selected products. No pricing engine/checkout formulas change. Campaign visibility or order does **not** reset catalogue prices; to end the demo sales, restore each desired regular price (the stored compare-at price) in the catalogue editor and clear its compare-at field. Reopening the database does not reapply the trial.

## Campaign landing and product grid

The home CTAs now open `/black-friday`: a red active heading above the campaign introduction and a tappable **Les bonnes affaires** feature photo. Its red badge and CTA lead to `/black-friday?view=products`, a two-column campaign-only gallery with portrait photos, red badges, catalogue brand/title, red current prices, struck reference prices and actual percentage savings. Signed-in users can use the existing catalogue favorites. No sample retailer names, stock counts or reference prices are copied.

The same CRM studio edits the home banner and rail background colors separately (contrasting text is picked automatically), both photos, page/card titles, badge, FR/AR CTAs and an optional campaign deadline. Deadline controls campaign visibility and the displayed countdown, **not** catalogue prices. Existing campaign JSON is read with defaults for the new fields, no DB schema migration or reseeding.

The inline price editor is available to CRM pricing users for products with a directly authored TND price. It updates `final_price` and `compare_at_price` through an audited, permission- and CSRF-protected write with stale-value checks; the card, product fiche and new cart additions all use the same catalogue price. Imported/cost-calculated prices remain controlled by the existing pricing workflow. An active campaign can only contain products with a photo, stock availability and a real discount. Saving layout, turning the campaign off or expiring it does not write prices.
