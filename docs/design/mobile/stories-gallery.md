# Mobile Stories gallery

Home → Stories **Explore** and each Home story card open `/stories`, not the Social tab. The optional `focus` parameter promotes the tapped card in the first section, without changing the CMS order of other cards. Tapping a gallery card uses `/stories?id=…` and the editorial Story viewer. Existing share URLs and public expiry rules are preserved.

The standalone gallery is #000000 including safe areas, with light status-bar glyphs and no white browser/navigation band. At 360 dp: 12 dp gutter, 336 × 504 dp featured portraits, ~278 × 418 dp cards for the snapping **Nouveau** rail, 8 dp horizontal and 12 dp vertical archive gaps, portraits in three columns alternating with two stacked small cards beside a larger one. Scrolling reveals a back-to-top button. The outer list is virtualized and the archive grows in batches of nine; **Voir plus de stories** is present only while there are remaining cards to show.

The gallery uses published, visible CMS Stories and existing authors' imagery, never the supplied reference screenshot assets. The archive here is a browsing index of the presently published Stories; it does not expose expired items. The latest-stories CTA follows the official Zayrovi Team account through the existing follow relation. It does not claim there is a Stories push campaign or grant any notification permission.

## Story player, articles and CRM

Opening a card (or its existing share link) now loads only that story via `GET /api/public/stories/:id/editorial`. The black viewer supports full-bleed, inset-media/editorial and product slides in authored order, with segmented progress, tap sides for navigation, play/pause, sound on video and close. It keeps one active video or GIF, starts muted, lets users change that preference on any slide (only video can play sound), and stops progression/playback when unfocused or backgrounded. Reduce Motion starts playback paused. A slide can open the card's own native article via **Lire** or **Découvrir**, or show a compact rectangle with the exact linked product, price and favorite. Back from an article or product returns to the story. The Social-tab viewer is unchanged.

The article has a 2:3 cover, white editorial content with a real author/date, ordered FR/AR paragraphs or images, and a horizontal rail of linked products. **Découvrir** also carries a black Zayrovi Team / Follow bar. Price, photos and favorites are based on live linked products. No reference article, prices, bylines or assets are bundled. Existing cards without a saved composition remain readable using their own cover/text; nothing is rewritten or automatically linked to a product. A missing, expired, future or unpublished story never falls through to a different article.

**CRM → Contenu → Stories officielles:** edit a card, associate products in order, then create a parcours. Add, remove and reorder 1–12 screens (photo/video/GIF), select a layout, enter FR/AR copy, choose a duration or use the default, select the linked product for a product screen, and assign **Lire** or **Découvrir**. Either CTA opens the same article for that card. The article editor can add up to 20 ordered text/image blocks and a selection title. Covers, article images and media may be uploaded or specified as direct URLs where the form provides a URL field. Publication/expiry and soft retirement continue using the existing Stories resource. The versioned `editorial_json` column is additive and deliberately omitted from the public gallery response. Content writes use the existing authentication, audit and transaction; invalid media and slide references fail before writing.

**CRM → Contenu → Social → Publications:** the current seven mobile layouts are available instead of the retired block-model editor. Existing rows and their legacy types are kept; selecting a new layout explicitly converts it. Retirement archives the record instead of deleting it.
